Castle defense

  • Mouse looks. W and S fly along the camera, A and D strafe. Space up, Shift down, double-tap W sprints.
  • The crosshair picks the ground. Keys 1–8 or the scroll wheel choose a building. Esc frees the mouse.
  • Walls are drawn: click a start, then click the other end. Aim near a wall tip to snap a joint. Longer walls cost less per piece.
  • Other buildings drop wherever you click on open grass, same as wall points. They will not overlap walls, the keep, or each other.
  • Farms make gold (max 6). Mage towers slow packs. Spike pits hurt anything that walks over them. A workshop repairs nearby walls and upgrades troops — upgraded troops wear better armor.
  • Click a building to inspect it, then upgrade that building. Walls, towers, farms, barracks, and workshops all gain stronger stats and a better look, up to level 3.
  • Open any barracks for a troop menu. Drag a box to select troops, then click the ground to send them. Q deselects, E selects all.
  • Build freely, then press Start waves. Survive 10 waves to win.
