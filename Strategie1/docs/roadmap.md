# Roadmap

The Maastrichtian theater and the national economy are playable. Later phases have to be playable and tested before the next one starts. None of the following are represented by decorative controls.

1. Done. Civilian and military construction, extraction of the six resources, harness production into the stockpile, and trade. Dockyards, shipbuilding, forts, rails, and blockades stay out of this phase.
2. Done. A pack designer whose statistics are the sum of its dinosaur species, plus recruitment that spends handlers and harness when both are on hand. Reinforcement of packs already in the field stays out of this phase.
3. Done. Fronts that hold gaps or attack across a shared border, plus armor, entrenchment, forts, weather, and encirclement in combat. Air power, planning bonus, and battle plans that draw arrows stay out of this phase.
4. Done. Rail, port, and hub logistics. Trucks reach two provinces from a hub. Railways and sea lanes carry supply farther, and the supply map draws those routes. Blockades stay out of this phase.
5. Done. Research slots, two doctrine schools, and original focus trees for the Rhenish Massif, Holy Cross Island, and the Carpathian Ridge. Finished work changes combat or factories. Air and naval research stay out of this phase.
6. Diplomacy, factions, wars as full objects, and occupation distinct from the control already tracked.
7. A hierarchical AI with a trace of why a country chose an action.
8. Air and naval systems that change combat and logistics.
9. More provinces, toward a world map, through the existing importer and renderer.
