Game overview

Castle defense

A browser game: fly over a meadow island, build around the keep in the middle, train an army, and hold off ten waves of attackers from the shore.

Play the game Minigames hub

What this is

Castle defense is one of three games on the minigames home page, next to Neon Clash and Mini 8-Ball. It runs in a web browser. There is no account and nothing to install.

The island

The map is a small island: grass, woods, beach, shallow water, rocks, and sea. The keep stands in the centre. Enemies arrive in the water around the edge.

Building and gold

The player draws wooden palisades and places farms, barracks, a workshop, spike pits, crossbows, cannons, and mage towers. Farms make gold. The workshop repairs walls and upgrades troops.

The waves

Ten waves: goblins, runners, archers, brutes, flying bats, wall climbers, and rams. They walk toward the keep. After a breach they keep hitting nearby wall pieces instead of rushing farms.

The army

Open a barracks to train spearman, slinger, raider, knight, or warden. Drag a box to select them, then click the ground to send them. Friendly troops can walk through the player’s own walls.

How it looks

The player flies around the island. Walls are wooden palisades that look the same from both sides. Soldiers look like small knights; enemies are green. They walk like people rather than sliding.

The HUD

Gold, army size, wave, and stronghold health sit at the top. The build bar along the bottom has eight buildings. Click a building in the world to inspect it, sell it, or upgrade troops.

How the game was built

  1. 1Castle defense was added to the minigames home page, next to the other two games.
  2. 2The meadow island, the keep, and a view the player can fly around came first.
  3. 3Next came building: spend gold on farms, towers, and walls drawn as a line on the grass.
  4. 4Then the attack: enemies land from the shore, walk to the keep, break walls, and the player wins or loses over ten waves.
  5. 5Then an army: train groups at the barracks, select them with a drag box, and send them out.
  6. 6Characters got a proper look — knights on the player’s side, green attackers — and they walk instead of sliding.
  7. 7Last polish: a clearer HUD, mage towers, spike pits, a workshop with troop upgrades, new attackers (bats, climbers, rams), and palisades that look the same from both sides.

How to try it

Get ready

Press Play, click the meadow so the mouse looks around, then fly with the keyboard. Place farms on grass for gold, barracks for soldiers, and walls and towers for defense. During this setup, gold cannot go above 540.

Train an army

Click a barracks and pick a troop. They appear and walk to the meeting point. Drag a box around soldiers to select them, then click the ground to send them. Q drops the selection. E selects everyone.

Start the waves

Enemies appear in the shallow water around the island. They walk from the beach, across the grass, toward the keep. If a wall is in the way, they attack the wall instead of going around it.

Defend

Crossbows shoot one enemy at a time. Cannons hit a small group nearby. Mage towers slow packs. Spike pits hurt walkers. Soldiers fight anything close enough.

Win or lose

If the keep’s health reaches zero, the game is lost. If wave 10 is cleared, the game is won. Defeated enemies give gold, and farms keep earning during play.

A building can be sold at any time to get the gold back and change the layout.
