# Site Null audio credits

Ambience (fluorescent buzz, rumble, hotel jazz, light-state beds) is synthesized in `js/audio.js`.
One-shots below are free samples, trimmed and resampled for the game.

## Kenney — Impact Sounds (CC0 1.0)

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Used as footsteps, metal/wood hits, and rock impacts:

- `foot_carpet_*.ogg` — footstep carpet 000–003
- `foot_concrete_*.ogg` — footstep concrete 000–003
- `foot_wood_*.ogg` — footstep wood 000–003
- `metal_*.ogg` — impactMetal light/medium
- `woodhit_*.ogg` — impactWood / impactPlank medium
- `rock_*.ogg` — impactMining

Public domain. Credit is optional.
